FAA EB 105A Vertiport Design Standards

Key Surfaces & Geometric Definitions

  • TLOF (Touchdown and Lift-Off Area): A load-bearing area centered within the FATO, typically sized to $1.0\,D$ where $D$ is the controlling aircraft dimension.
  • FATO (Final Approach and Take-Off Area): An obstacle-free area sized to at least $1.5\,D$ (or $2.0\,D$) over which the final phase of approach or start of takeoff maneuver is completed.
  • Safety Area (SA): Extends around the FATO out to $2.0\,D$ to protect aircraft during operational excursions.
  • Takeoff Climb & Approach Surface (OLS): An inclined volumetric plane beginning at the edge of the Safety Area, sloping upwards at a customizable ratio (default 8:1 = 12.5% or steeper for eVTOLs) with a 15% lateral flare divergence.
  • Transitional Surfaces: Lateral side surfaces extending outwards and upwards at a 2:1 slope from the Safety Area perimeter to the edge of the approach surfaces.

Elevated Vertiports & Greenfield Toggle

When a vertiport is elevated (above 0m AGL), enabling the Greenfield Building toggle generates a 3D structural building directly underneath the pad from the terrain. If placing on top of an existing rooftop, disable the toggle to view the elevated envelope without obstructing the OpenStreetMap 3D building.

Default Aircraft: Wisk Gen 6

The Wisk Gen 6 is the world's leading autonomous 4-passenger eVTOL with a 50-foot (15.24 m) controlling dimension ($D$), featuring 12 proprietary lift/thrust rotors.
